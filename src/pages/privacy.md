Last updated: 2026/06/30

ZSUN uses this website to share information about club rides, racing, coaching, resources and joining the community.

We do not sell personal information.

We may receive personal information if you contact us directly, join linked community spaces, or use external services linked from this website. This may include your name, email address, Discord details, Zwift-related details, or the information you choose to provide.

We use that information only to respond to you, support club activity, help with ride or race participation, or provide coaching-related information where relevant.

This website links to third-party services, including Discord, Zwift, ZwiftPower, WTRL, ZwiftRacing.app, Ladder League and the ZSUN Performance Hub. Those services have their own privacy practices and are responsible for how they handle your information.

If analytics, forms, cookies or mailing lists are added later, this policy should be updated.

To ask about privacy or request removal of information you have provided to ZSUN, contact us through the Contact page or the ZSUN Discord community.
